# Phonology

The source §2 defines 28 letters, eight vowels, four diphthongs and normally penultimate stress. `ñ` is /ɲ/, `ŋ` /ŋ/; `ı` and `i` differ. Three adjacent vowels or consonants are disallowed in proposed forms. The validator reports mechanical problems, not pronunciation quality.
