# Owner decisions AD-2026-001 and AD-2026-002

On 2026-11-05 the language owner explicitly adopted **Alei** as the official human-language name and **Mirav** / **Miraven** as the preferred help root and verb for language v1.1.0. `Alei!` continues to mean hello. Machine-readable records are in [2026-11-05.json](2026-11-05.json). The earlier conversation transcript was not independently recovered; the owner's present decision is direct authorization and the earlier history remains owner-reported evidence. The implementation is on `owner/alei-v1.1`; the review is [PR #8](https://github.com/egecagintepe/alei-language/pull/8), with its implementation commit recorded in the machine-readable decisions.

The immutable v1.0 source, extraction and source IDs retain `Mivav`, `Mivaven` and `Mivavar`. In the v1.1.0 interpretation view, `Mivav` and `Mivaven` are legacy aliases with warnings; they do not add canonical roots. `Mivavar` is historical with its v1.1 status unresolved. `Miravar` is not adopted. Neither the F1/F2/F3 AleiFlow experiments nor the experimental L4 compositions are approved by these decisions.
